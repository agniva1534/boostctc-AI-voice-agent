# BoostCTC Customize Your Learning Experience

URL: https://boostctc.com/professional/customize-experience

## Page Title
"Customize Your Learning Experience"
Subtitle: "Choose a preset or customize your own configuration"

## Section 1: Exercise Topic and Topic Order

### Presets
Users can choose from three presets:
1. **Engineer** — Optimized for engineering professionals
2. **Middle/High School Student** — Optimized for younger learners (currently selected in example)
3. **Custom** — Fully customizable configuration

### Topic Configuration
Each topic appears as a draggable block (reorderable). For each topic:
- **Topic name** (removable tag)
- **Frequency** dropdown (0, 1, 2, or 3 exercises per cycle)
- **Delete button** to remove the topic entirely
- **Drag handle** to reorder topics

### Available Topics (in current "Middle/High School Student" preset order)
1. AI Literacy (Frequency: 1)
2. Personal Productivity and Systems (Frequency: 1)
3. Innovation & Creativity (Frequency: 1)
4. Interview Preparation (Frequency: 1)
5. Emotional Development (Frequency: 1)
6. Leadership and People Management (Frequency: 1)
7. Social and Cultural Intelligence (Frequency: 1)

### Rotation Explanation
The system explains the rotation cycle:
- "First you will get 1 exercise of AI Literacy"
- "Next you will get 1 exercise of Personal Productivity and Systems"
- (continues through all topics in order)
- Then cycles back to the beginning

### + Add More
Button to add additional topics to the rotation.

## Section 2: Exercise Format

Three format options:

### 1. Paragraph Only
- 0% Reading Comprehension
- 100% Passage
- Pure passage-based exercises

### 2. Balanced Mix (Recommended)
- 50% Reading Comprehension
- 50% Passage
- Selected by default; tagged with "Recommended" badge

### 3. Reading Focused
- 70% Reading Comprehension
- 30% Passage
- More emphasis on comprehension-based exercises

## Report Issue
A floating button at the bottom right allows users to report issues with the platform.
