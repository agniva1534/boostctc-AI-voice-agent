# About TalkValley Technologies LLC

Empowering students with critical thinking and communication skills through innovative AI-powered learning

Also known asBoostCTC

## Our Mission

To equip every student—regardless of background—with the skills to think critically, communicate clearly, and thrive in a fast-changing world.

We believe that strong reading comprehension and expression are the foundation for lifelong learning. BoostCTC blends cognitive science, AI feedback, and classroom simplicity to make those skills accessible, measurable, and motivating.
