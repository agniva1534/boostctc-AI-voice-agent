## Our Commitment to Privacy, Safety, and Transparency

At TalkValley Technologies LLC, (dba BoostCTC), we take our responsibility to students, parents, and educators seriously. This page outlines our key policies and compliance measures that ensure your data and your rights are protected.

## Privacy Policy

- We do not sell or rent user data.
- We only collect the data necessary to deliver personalized learning experiences and analytics.
- Student data is used solely for educational purposes.
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Children's Data (COPPA & FERPA Compliance)

- We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).
- Parental or school consent is obtained before collecting personal information from children under 13.
- Schools and parents can request data deletion at any time.

## Messaging Compliance (WhatsApp & Others)

- We use messaging services like WhatsApp only after obtaining clear opt-in from users.
- Parents and educators can opt out at any time.
- Messaging content is limited to educational updates such as student progress, class summaries, or alerts.
- All WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted.

[View WhatsApp Messaging Policy](https://boostctc.com/whatsapp-policy)
