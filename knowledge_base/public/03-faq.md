## 4\. How does BoostCTC handle WhatsApp messages?

**Answer:**

BoostCTC uses WhatsApp to send educational updates, such as student progress and class summaries. Parents and educators can opt out at any time, and all WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted.

For more information, visit: [WhatsApp Messaging Policy](https://boostctc.com/whatsapp-policy)

## 5\. How does BoostCTC ensure compliance with privacy laws like COPPA and FERPA?

**Answer:**

BoostCTC complies with COPPA and FERPA by obtaining parental or school consent before collecting data from children under 13. Parents and schools also have the right to request data deletion.

For more information, visit: [Legal Compliance](https://boostctc.com/legal)
